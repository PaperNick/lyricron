The Keyboard-First Lyric Timing Workflow

The fastest way to time lyrics is to never reach for the mouse. Here's the one-pass, keyboard-only flow and the full shortcut reference behind it.

Sep 24, 2026 5 min read

The one-pass loop

The whole workflow is a short loop you repeat once per line: press Space to play, hit Enter the instant the next line starts, and let the track keep rolling. Miss a line? Backspace undoes the last stamp without stopping the music. Slightly early or late? [ and ] nudge the last line by 50 ms while playback continues.

Because you never stop the song, a three-minute track takes about three minutes to time. The moment you notice a line is off, you fix it in place and keep going.

Moving around without the mouse

Seek with the arrow keys: ← / → jump five seconds, and Shift + ← / Shift + → step one second at a time for fine positioning. To jump straight to a timed line, Ctrl/⌘ + ← and Ctrl/⌘ + → move to the previous and next stamped line.

Why keyboard-first wins

It's not just speed. Tapping a key has lower latency than clicking, so your stamps land closer to the true beat. And because the hand stays on the keyboard, undoing and redoing becomes reflexive, which means you experiment more freely. The result is a tighter sync with less effort. Every shortcut is also remappable through the app's settings if a key doesn't suit your setup.
