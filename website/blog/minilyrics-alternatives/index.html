MiniLyrics Is Gone: The Best Alternatives in 2026

MiniLyrics was the go-to for synced lyrics on the desktop for years, then it vanished. If you're looking for a replacement, here's where to go in 2026.

Oct 4, 2026 6 min read

What happened to MiniLyrics

MiniLyrics was a Windows utility that floated a synced-lyrics window over your music player. It was built on a proprietary lyrics service, and when that service went dark, the app stopped being able to fetch lyrics and gradually faded out. The core idea, synced lyrics beside your player, is still very much alive; it just moved into the players themselves.

Option 1: players with built-in synced lyrics

The modern replacement for MiniLyrics is usually a player that reads LRC natively, so you don't need a separate overlay at all. The big four:

  • Musicolet (Android) - offline-first, reads LRC with no setup. Setup guide.
  • VLC (all platforms) - has a lyrics view you enable once. Setup guide.
  • foobar2000 (Windows) - lyrics panel via a component. Setup guide.
  • Poweramp (Android) - synced lyrics with LRC files. Setup guide.

Option 2: make your own synced files

MiniLyrics' real value wasn't the window, it was the database of timed lyrics. When a synced file doesn't exist for your track, the answer is to make one yourself. Lyricron does this in the browser: load the audio, add the lyrics, tap through once, and export a standard .lrc any of the players above will read. No install, no account, and nothing uploads anywhere.

Why the no-install approach wins

MiniLyrics required a desktop install and a running service. The modern workflow is lighter: open a web page, time the song, download a file. There's no software to keep updated and no service to go dark on you again. The argument for keeping everything local is laid out in why your lyrics tool shouldn't need your song files.

Which to choose

  • Just want lyrics to show? Pick the player you already use and follow its LRC guide above.
  • Need a synced file that doesn't exist yet? Make it with Lyricron, then drop it in.
  • Karaoke at home? Making karaoke files at home covers the end-to-end flow.
